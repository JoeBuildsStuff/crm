## Design Context

### Users
Solo founders managing relationship-heavy pipelines.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, focused, dependable.
The product should evoke calm clarity under load so users can move decisively without feeling rushed or overwhelmed.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-first interface with minimal, premium restraint and strong typography inspired by `cursor.com`.
Use muted cool neutrals with a restrained accent color, and avoid loud dashboard tropes.
Anti-references: loud SaaS dashboards, gradient-heavy crypto aesthetics, and cluttered card farms.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize relational continuity: keep contact context, bios, and recent touchpoints immediately legible.
2. Preserve momentum: make next steps obvious and reduce friction for capture, planning, and follow-through.
3. Design for calm focus: favor restrained contrast, clear hierarchy, and minimal visual noise.
4. Use purposeful motion only: avoid decorative animation and keep transitions subtle and informative.
5. Maintain practical readability: no mandated WCAG bar, but preserve readable contrast and avoid unnecessary motion.

### Use Cases
- Track contacts.
- Capture bios and relationship context.
- Define and close next steps.
- Maintain pipeline momentum over time.
